## Phase Goal

Verbatim from `.planning/ROADMAP.md` §Phase 4. Not in `As a … I want to … so that …` form and not
rewritten here — run `/gsd mvp-phase 4` if the three-slot form is wanted.

> Groups run the ban ritual their own way on one shared screen, and nobody sees what they should
> not have seen

**Slice delivered by this plan:** a host opens the config screen, picks `Snake`, types `2` bans per
player, taps `Start draft`, and the shared screen says `Pass 1 of 2 — Sam bans` over the roster.
That is a thing nobody could do before this plan and the first user-visible step of the phase.

<objective>
Give blind and snake a start seam of their own, put the two new fields on the config screen, and
make the ban stage a fourth screen.

Purpose: D-01 deliberately buys two seams. `hostBanlist` keeps `createTournament` atomic and
byte-for-byte the path Phase 2 verified; blind and snake get a sibling that dispatches
`schedule/compiled` then `draft/started` with no pool. And Pitfall 4 is why the stage is a fourth
`Screen` member rather than a mode inside the draft screen: `selectPhase` answers `'cards'` with an
empty pool and a resolved order, so a draft-screen mode would put the app into card play on a screen
with no pool.

Output: `createBanStage`, two config controls, a fourth `Screen`, and a stage shell that branches on
`selectBanStageState`.
</objective>
