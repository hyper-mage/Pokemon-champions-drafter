## Phase Goal

Verbatim from `.planning/ROADMAP.md` §Phase 4. Not in `As a … I want to … so that …` form and not
rewritten here — run `/gsd mvp-phase 4` if the three-slot form is wanted.

> Groups run the ban ritual their own way on one shared screen, and nobody sees what they should
> not have seen

**Slice delivered by this plan:** the host, halfway down a Discord message, types the wrong Pokémon
for player three — and can take it back. That is the correction path for the phase's primary input
method, and D-03 calls it mandatory rather than convenient.

<objective>
Make every ban action undoable on the one existing stack, and close the live-region leak that
widening `UndoRemoval` opens.

Purpose: 04-RESEARCH calls the exhaustive-`never` rewrite "the single highest-value change in the
phase". `store.ts:416-447` is a chain of `if` arms ending in an **unguarded** `return` that
interpolates a species name. A new `UndoRemoval.kind` that gets no arm above it lands there and
announces the species a host just privately removed — in a room full of people, through a channel
the visual shield does not cover — and it compiles, type-checks and passes every existing test.

Output: three `UndoRemoval` kinds, an exhaustive announcement, and three confirm copy sets.
</objective>
