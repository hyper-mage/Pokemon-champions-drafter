## Phase Goal

Verbatim from `.planning/ROADMAP.md` §Phase 4. Not in `As a … I want to … so that …` form and not
rewritten here — run `/gsd mvp-phase 4` if the three-slot form is wanted.

> Groups run the ban ritual their own way on one shared screen, and nobody sees what they should
> not have seen

**Slice delivered by this plan:** a host who configures 8 players, blind mode and 2 bans each on a
pool sized to the whole legal roster is told *before anyone bans anything* that the pool cannot be
drawn — instead of reaching the reveal and watching `Start draft` throw a `RangeError` on the shared
screen mid-ritual.

<objective>
Make the config-time feasibility gate fully pessimistic about player bans, and close the crash path
that pessimism about Megas alone leaves open.

Purpose: 04-RESEARCH calls this "the single most important correction in this document after the
`undoAnnouncement` fall-through". D-21 names one predicate; the reachable failure is a second one,
and it is a throw rather than a block. `04-UI-SPEC` §7's claim that the post-reveal blocked state is
"reachable only from an imported or hand-edited document" becomes true when this plan lands, and is
false until it does.

Output: two new input fields, three new blocking codes, a `q` term on three predicates, and one
two-arm message composer.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

|----------|-------------|
| host-typed `Bans per player` → the gate | A free numeric input. `<input type="number">` yields an empty field as `null` and every relational comparison with `NaN` is false, so a gate that merely compared would report all-clear on a broken config. |
