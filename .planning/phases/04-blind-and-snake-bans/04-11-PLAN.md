## Phase Goal

Verbatim from `.planning/ROADMAP.md` §Phase 4. Not in `As a … I want to … so that …` form and not
rewritten here — run `/gsd mvp-phase 4` if the three-slot form is wanted.

> Groups run the ban ritual their own way on one shared screen, and nobody sees what they should
> not have seen

**Slice delivered by this plan:** the host taps `Reveal bans`, the room reads six players' bans at
once with the collisions named out loud, and then — on a second, deliberate tap — the pool is drawn
and the draft begins. This is the last surface of the phase and the moment the ritual pays off.

<objective>
Show every ban at once with attribution, name the collisions, re-check feasibility, and draw the pool
on a separate tap.

Purpose: three decisions converge here and each one is load-bearing. D-13 keeps attribution because
"Sam banned Garchomp" is the entire social payoff of blind mode. D-19 names collisions explicitly
rather than silently absorbing them, which is what makes BAN-07's `bothApply` branch a visible rule
rather than an invisible one. And D-23 separates the reveal from the draw so the group reads the
reveal before the screen changes under them, and a failed RULE-08 check never has to un-draw
anything.

Output: `BanReveal`, `drawPoolForBanStage`, the checkpoint split, and the phase's mandatory physical
pass.
</objective>
