## Phase Goal

Verbatim from `.planning/ROADMAP.md` §Phase 4. Not in `As a … I want to … so that …` form and not
rewritten here — run `/gsd mvp-phase 4` if the three-slot form is wanted.

> Groups run the ban ritual their own way on one shared screen, and nobody sees what they should
> not have seen

**Slice delivered by this plan:** a host picks `Blind`, starts, and the shared screen says
`Sam is next` over `0 of 6 entered` and a list of players — and nothing else. The room can stare at
it for an hour and learn nothing. That mode has been a disabled option since Phase 2; this plan is
where it becomes selectable.

<objective>
Build the blind stage's resting destination, and close the top bar's disclosure so it cannot leak a
submitted ban.

Purpose: `04-UI-SPEC` §4 calls this "the phase's most important screen and deliberately the plainest
— a room glancing at it must be able to tell from three metres that there is nothing on it." The
second half is subtler: `TopBar.tsx:209-217` renders every banned name behind a native `<summary>`
anyone in the room can open with one click, and left unchanged it exposes every submitted ban before
the reveal. 04-RESEARCH names it the least obvious leak channel in the phase.

Output: `BlindLocked`, the `'blindLocked'` arm, Amendment 1's disclosure narrowing, and `Blind`
enabled.
</objective>
