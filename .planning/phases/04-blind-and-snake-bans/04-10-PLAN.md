## Phase Goal

Verbatim from `.planning/ROADMAP.md` §Phase 4. Not in `As a … I want to … so that …` form and not
rewritten here — run `/gsd mvp-phase 4` if the three-slot form is wanted.

> Groups run the ban ritual their own way on one shared screen, and nobody sees what they should
> not have seen

**Slice delivered by this plan:** the host taps `Enter Sam's bans`, the screen becomes nothing but
Sam's ban entry, they type two names off Discord, tap `Lock in Sam's bans`, and the screen is back to
`1 of 6 entered` with nothing on it. Every other way out of that surface — the panic button, an
alt-tab, a browser Back and Forward — lands in exactly the same place with nothing retained.

<objective>
Build the full-screen entry surface and the lifecycle guard that makes every exit from it safe.

Purpose: BAN-05 and BAN-06 are the phase's real acceptance risk, and the ROADMAP flags BAN-06 as
"easy to get silently wrong". It is worse than that: 04-RESEARCH **measured** that the obvious test
passes while proving nothing, because `new PageTransitionEvent('pageshow', { persisted: true })`
yields `persisted === undefined` under the installed happy-dom. A gate that cannot fail is not a
gate.

Output: `installBanShield`, `BlindEntry`, the `'blindEntry'` arm, and a bfcache test that actually
tests something.
</objective>
