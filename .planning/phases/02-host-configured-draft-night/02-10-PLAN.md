  <what-built>
    Plan 02-09 made the pool pane's expand control always render. While a draft is running it
    is inert: dimmed, marked `aria-disabled="true"`, still focusable, and followed by the
    reason `Available once the draft is complete`. Clicking it is refused — the pane stays
    split and the board stays on screen. Once the draft finishes the same control becomes
    live and the reason disappears.

    `.pane__chrome` also now reserves `min-height: var(--target-min)`, so a chrome slot with
    no control in it holds its height instead of collapsing to zero. That collapse is what
    made the pool's grid start about 44px above the board's and read as a failed render.

    The rule itself did not change. The pool still cannot be expanded mid-draft, because
    doing so would collapse the board to a strip.
  </what-built>

  <how-to-verify>
    Run `npm run dev` and open the printed URL.

    **Setup.** Start a new tournament with two players and any valid pool, then `Start draft`
    and make one pick so the board is not empty.

    1. **The affordance exists and explains itself.** Look at the top of the pool pane, above
       the grid. You should see an `Expand the pool` button, visibly dimmed, with
       `Available once the draft is complete` beside it. Confirm you can tell at a glance
       that it is unavailable on purpose rather than broken.

    2. **The panes line up.** Compare the top edge of the pool grid with the top edge of the
       draft board. They should start on the same line. This is the specific thing that
       looked wrong before — the pool's content sat roughly one button-height higher than the
       board's.

    3. **The control is genuinely refused.** Click `Expand the pool`. Nothing should happen:
       the layout does not change, the pool does not take the full width, and the board stays
       exactly where it is at its normal size.

    4. **Keyboard reaches the explanation.** Press Tab until focus lands on
       `Expand the pool`. It must be reachable and show a focus ring — this is deliberate, so
       a keyboard or screen-reader user can reach the reason. Press Enter or Space; again
       nothing should happen.

    5. **The board control still works.** Click `Expand the draft board`. The board should
       take the full width and the pool should collapse to a narrow strip offering
       `Show the pool`. Click that to come back. Confirm the two panes are still aligned
       after returning.

    6. **It goes live when the draft ends.** Complete the draft — with 2 players and 6 rounds
       that is 12 picks. On the completed screen the pool side shows the export panels.
       Confirm `Expand the pool` is now at full strength with NO reason text beside it, and
       that clicking it now does expand the pool to full width.

    Point 6 is the control case: if the button looks identical in steps 1 and 6, the inert
    styling is not landing and that is a failure worth reporting.
  </how-to-verify>
