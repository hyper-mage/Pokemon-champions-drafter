## Phase Goal

Verbatim from `.planning/ROADMAP.md` §Phase 5. Not in `As a … I want to … so that …` form and not
rewritten here — run `/gsd mvp-phase 5` if the three-slot form is wanted.

> The night runs past the draft — round robin, a seeded cut, a single-elimination bracket, results
> recorded and correctable — and the finished tournament stays readable and re-runnable after the
> Champions roster rotates

**Slice delivered by this plan:** the round robin produces a table people can argue with and a cut
that carries it into the bracket. After this plan a host can read the standings, see which link put
each player where, hand-order the players the tool refused to separate, and take a top-N cut.

<precedence>
`05-UI-SPEC.md:44` states "There is no `05-RESEARCH.md`." It is dated a day earlier. **RESEARCH
governs mechanism; UI-SPEC governs copy, layout and tokens.** §6, §7, §8 and §Copywriting are the
specification for everything visible here.

**Two rulings carried forward, both already made and both rendered here for the first time.**

1. **A still-tied block shares one position number** (ruled in 05-03). Three players tied for third
   render `3 3 3`, not `3 4 5`, and the next resolved row renders `6`. `3 4 5` would assert an order
   the tool has explicitly refused to compute, contradicting the row's own
   `Tied — order these yourself` note.
2. **The cut's second inert reason is copy this planner supplies**, because `05-UI-SPEC.md` §8 gates
   only on completeness and RESEARCH Pitfall 4 shows completeness does not imply resolution. Ruled in
   05-06, rendered here, byte-for-byte:

   > `The cut at {n} splits a tie. Order the tied players yourself before you take it.`

   It follows the shape of the contract's own blocked reason — states the problem, names the next
   action, second person, present tense, no exclamation.
</precedence>

<objective>
Render the standings, the override and the cut on the tournament screen's round-robin stage.

Purpose: `05-UI-SPEC.md` §Pure-core boundary is blunt about the division — "If a surface here seems to
need the UI to decide a rule, the selector is missing. Add the selector." Every rule these three
components display already exists in `src/core/tournament.ts`. Their job is to render it, to make the
inert states reachable by keyboard, and to keep the one number the host types bounded by the same
constant the guard uses.

Output: three components, three stylesheets, and the mounting that turns the round-robin stage into a
screen a host can finish.
</objective>
