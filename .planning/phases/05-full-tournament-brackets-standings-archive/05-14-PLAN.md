## Phase Goal

Verbatim from `.planning/ROADMAP.md` §Phase 5. Not in `As a … I want to … so that …` form and not
rewritten here — run `/gsd mvp-phase 5` if the three-slot form is wanted.

> The night runs past the draft — round robin, a seeded cut, a single-elimination bracket, results
> recorded and correctable — and the finished tournament stays readable and re-runnable after the
> Champions roster rotates

**Slice delivered by this plan:** the night becomes readable as a story. After this plan a host opens
the recap from the bracket — or from a completed draft-only night — and reads the whole evening in
order, including the bans, the cards, every pick, the swaps, the results and the corrections.

<precedence>
`05-UI-SPEC.md:44` states "There is no `05-RESEARCH.md`." It is dated a day earlier. **RESEARCH
governs mechanism; UI-SPEC governs copy, layout and tokens.** §11 supplies the sections, the ten entry
lines and the two correction marks; RESEARCH §The Recap Needs The Log supplies the signature and the
three module-header rules.

**The recap is a doc-taking function, not a selector.** `05-RESEARCH.md` calls this "the single
structural finding a planner is most likely to miss", and Pitfall 8 gives its warning sign: the
`Round robin` section has exactly as many lines as there are matches, no matter how many corrections
the log holds. Every existing selector takes `DraftState`; `DraftState` holds only the latest result
per match, because that is what D-09's fold arm does. `state` is passed **alongside** the document
because D-21 requires the ban section to read `DraftState` selectors.

**The recap is rendered, not exported.** `05-CONTEXT.md` §Deferred: a copy-to-clipboard or shareable
text form of the recap was raised while designing D-19/D-20 and **not pursued** — PERS-09 says the
recap is *rendered*, and the export surface in this project is species-only pastes for two specific
targets. Do not add a copy button, a text export or a print stylesheet.
</precedence>

<objective>
Fold the log into a chronological account of the night, and render it.

Purpose: D-19 chose chronological because it is closest to what the log actually is, so the fold is
close to a formatting pass and it reads as a story of the evening rather than a table of outcomes.
D-20 makes coverage total, D-21 makes the ban section honour Phase 4's secrecy contract on a new
surface over the same log, and D-22 makes the corrections visible — which is what makes this the
honest record when somebody asks why the bracket does not look the way they remember.

Output: one pure doc-taking module, one list component, and the two entry points.
</objective>

```ts
// src/core/recap.ts — NEW module, mirroring undo.ts's doc-taking convention
export type RecapSection = 'bans' | 'cards' | 'round' | 'swaps' | 'roundRobin' | 'bracket';

export interface RecapEntry {
  section: RecapSection;
  /** Present only for `section: 'round'`. */
  round?: number;
  seq: number;
  kind: 'ban' | 'collision' | 'card' | 'pick' | 'swap' | 'pass' | 'match' | 'cut' | 'override' | 'void';
  /** Ids and names the line interpolates. No formatted prose — that is the component's job. */
  playerIds: readonly string[];
  monIds: readonly string[];
  /** D-22. A superseded result carries `correctedLater`; its replacement carries `corrects`. */
  correction: 'none' | 'correctedLater' | 'corrects';
  detail: { winnerGames?: number; loserGames?: number; metric?: number; value?: number; count?: number };
}

export function buildRecap(doc: TournamentDoc, state: DraftState): readonly RecapEntry[];
```
