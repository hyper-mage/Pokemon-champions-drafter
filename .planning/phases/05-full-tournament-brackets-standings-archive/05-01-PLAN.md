## Phase Goal

Verbatim from `.planning/ROADMAP.md` §Phase 5. Not in `As a … I want to … so that …` form and not
rewritten here — run `/gsd mvp-phase 5` if the three-slot form is wanted.

> The night runs past the draft — round robin, a seeded cut, a single-elimination bracket, results
> recorded and correctable — and the finished tournament stays readable and re-runnable after the
> Champions roster rotates

**Slice delivered by this plan:** the document learns to hold a tournament. Three config fields, four
fold fields, one migration and one gate. Nothing in this phase can be recorded, folded, seeded or
rendered until the document can carry it, and STATE.md decision 4 records that a missed schema site
fails silently and invisibly to import-only tests.

<precedence>
**Two upstream conflicts, ruled here and restated in every plan they touch.**

1. `05-UI-SPEC.md:44` states "There is no `05-RESEARCH.md`." That was true on 2026-08-25.
   `05-RESEARCH.md` is dated 2026-08-26 and reconciles itself to the contract. **RESEARCH governs
   mechanism; UI-SPEC governs copy, layout and tokens.** Where this plan quotes a sentence a host
   reads, the UI-SPEC is the source and it is byte-for-byte.
2. The library's storage is a **new `src/adapters/library.ts` with its own key**, not a reshaped
   `persistence.ts`. Ruled in 05-09; noted here because `persistence.ts` is deliberately **not** in
   this plan's `files_modified` — it asks `SUPPORTED_SCHEMA_VERSIONS` rather than comparing
   `SCHEMA_VERSION`, so the bump moves it by definition.
</precedence>

<objective>
Bump the tournament document to schema 5 and give the feasibility gate its one new sentence.

Purpose: `config.depth` has been stored on every document since schema 2 and read by nothing.
D-01 is what starts, and it needs three companions — the TOUR-07 metric (D-04) and a best-of format
per stage (D-08) — plus four `DraftState` fields for the fold to land in. Every one of these is a
string-literal union or a plain array written into files that later builds read back, so each gets
the comment-per-member treatment `BanMode` and `RoundKind` already carry.

Output: schema 5, a lossless 4 → 5 migration, three validated config fields, four fold fields, and
`bracketNeedsFourPlayers`.
</objective>
