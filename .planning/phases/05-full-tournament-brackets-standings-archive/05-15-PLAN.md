## Phase Goal

Verbatim from `.planning/ROADMAP.md` §Phase 5. Not in `As a … I want to … so that …` form and not
rewritten here — run `/gsd mvp-phase 5` if the three-slot form is wanted.

> The night runs past the draft — round robin, a seeded cut, a single-elimination bracket, results
> recorded and correctable — and the finished tournament stays readable and re-runnable after the
> Champions roster rotates

**Slice delivered by this plan:** the phase is checked against the one criterion no test can reach. A
group reads a shared screen from across a room, and this phase put 28 cells on it. DRFT-14 is an
acceptance criterion for the whole shared screen, not a polish item.

<precedence>
`05-UI-SPEC.md:44` states "There is no `05-RESEARCH.md`." It is dated a day earlier. **RESEARCH
governs mechanism; UI-SPEC governs copy, layout and tokens.** DRFT-14's assertions and the physical
check's script are `05-UI-SPEC.md` §DRFT-14's, verbatim.

**RULING — the fifth physical check is run in this phase, batched with Phase 4's outstanding one.**
`05-RESEARCH.md` §Environment Availability leaves the choice to the planner and records both facts:
check 5 has no automated substitute and gates DRFT-14 for every new surface here, and Phase 4's pass
(`04-11` task 3) is still outstanding and recorded in `.planning/STATE.md` as **BLOCKING**. Batching is
the right call because the rig is identical — 8 players, `blind` bans, `standard` density, a ~24" 1080p
screen and three metres of floor — so running them together costs one setup instead of two and one
interruption of the host instead of two.

**The remedy set for a legibility failure is closed.** `.planning/STATE.md`'s Phase 4 blocker note and
`05-UI-SPEC.md` §Typography both record the standing prohibition: a failure at 24px is a finding about
`--text-heading` on a crowded shared surface and is **not a licence to introduce a fifth font size**.
The three remedies available inside the contract are the existing four-size scale, removing the metric
from the cell (leaving it in the standings only), and raising `--results-col-min` at the cost of the
no-scroll threshold dropping from 8 players to 7. Anything else is a contract change and goes back to
the owner.
</precedence>

<objective>
Close the phase: assert everything a machine can assert, then put a person three metres from a screen.

Purpose: this phase introduces one new legibility risk and it is **density of information rather than
size**. Every size here already passed a physical check in Phase 3 or 4 — the champion's 36px is the
priority-card digit, every player name's 24px is the hand-strip pip, every 18px line is the Mega
marker. What is new is 28 cells in one view where the ban board had at most 8 columns of 8. That is
exactly why the remaining count is in words at 24px and unplayed cells are visually empty: neither
depends on resolving 28 small differences across a room.

Output: the automatable assertions run, and `05-HUMAN-UAT.md` recording the human result per surface.
</objective>
