## Phase Goal

Verbatim from `.planning/ROADMAP.md` §Phase 5. Not in `As a … I want to … so that …` form and not
rewritten here — run `/gsd mvp-phase 5` if the three-slot form is wanted.

> The night runs past the draft — round robin, a seeded cut, a single-elimination bracket, results
> recorded and correctable — and the finished tournament stays readable and re-runnable after the
> Champions roster rotates

**Slice delivered by this plan:** a group can play the round robin and record it. After this plan a
real person can leave the completed draft, see every pairing, record a winner with games and a number,
watch the count go down, and correct a result they got wrong — end to end, through the log, with undo.

<precedence>
`05-UI-SPEC.md:44` states "There is no `05-RESEARCH.md`." It is dated a day earlier. **RESEARCH
governs mechanism; UI-SPEC governs copy, layout and tokens.** This plan is mostly the latter, so every
string, size and layout figure comes from `05-UI-SPEC.md` §4, §5, §Layout Budget and §Copywriting
byte-for-byte, and the two dispatches come from RESEARCH §Corrections as Appends.

**RULING — the tournament surfaces get a fifth `Screen` union member, entered by an explicit host
action and left by one. `screenForState` is NOT changed to route into it.**

RESEARCH Open Question 1 leaves this to planning and recommends the fifth member. Both halves of the
recommendation are adopted and both matter:

- **A fifth member rather than a mode inside `draft`.** `05-UI-SPEC.md` §Layout Budget forces
  `.draft-shell` (full-bleed): the 8-player crosstable needs 204px columns and `.app-shell`'s
  1200px cap yields 114px, which cannot hold `--results-col-min`'s 188px. The `Screen` doc block at
  `app.tsx:127-154` already argues that the ban stage earned a fourth member because a mode inside the
  draft screen would have to shield five selectors individually; the same arithmetic applies here.
- **Not auto-routed.** Routing on `selectIsTournamentComplete` would yank the host off the per-player
  export panels the instant the last pick lands, which is wrong. `CompletedDraft` already carries the
  posture — it replaces the pool grid and nothing else so `Undo last move` stays reachable — and the
  entry control inherits it. No log action is involved: the round robin exists the moment the draft
  completes, because every pairing is derived.

**D-10's two dispatches are followed literally** (05-08's ruling). `causedBySeq` is read back off
`getDoc()` between them, on `app.tsx:514`'s precedent — "Re-read: the play is in the log now".
</precedence>

<objective>
Put the round robin on a screen and let the host record it.

Purpose: `05-UI-SPEC.md` §The reframe is the constraint every decision here inherits — nothing in the
tool tells the group what to play next, so the grid itself has to make "what is left" obvious at a
glance. That rules out the first thing a bracket implementation reaches for. **This phase adds no
accent-marked "next" indicator and the executor must not build one.**

Output: a stage shell, a crosstable, a record dialog, and a fifth route.
</objective>
