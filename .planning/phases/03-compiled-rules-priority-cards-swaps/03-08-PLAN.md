## Phase Goal

Verbatim from `.planning/ROADMAP.md` §Phase 3 — this Goal line is not in `As a … I want to … so
that …` form and was not rewritten here; run `/gsd mvp-phase 3` if the three-slot form is wanted.

> The draft becomes the one the spec describes — composition requirements compile into a round
> schedule that types the team slots, players bid priority cards for turn order over that visible
> schedule, and swaps can only take something the target slot allows.

**Slice delivered by this plan:** the round opens, the pool pane becomes a card panel, each player
in turn puts a card down face up, the last card resolves the order, the panel disappears and the
sticky head reads `Pick order: 1 Ada · 2 Bo · 3 Cy` for the whole of the picking that follows.

<objective>
Put the card-play step on the shared screen and make the phase the one place the screen's mode is
decided.

Purpose: D-17's state boundary is what makes "played but not yet resolved" unrepresentable. It also
rewrites `selectCurrentTurn`, which three callers read — and all three are wrong during card play
unless they are fixed in the same change.

Output: `selectPhase`, a rewritten `selectCurrentTurn` and its three blast-radius fixes, `CardFace`,
`CardPanel`, the sticky head's phase line, and two new pane-availability rows.
</objective>
