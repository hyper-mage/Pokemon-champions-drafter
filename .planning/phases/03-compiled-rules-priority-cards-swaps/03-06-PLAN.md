## Phase Goal

Verbatim from `.planning/ROADMAP.md` §Phase 3 — this Goal line is not in `As a … I want to … so
that …` form and was not rewritten here; run `/gsd mvp-phase 3` if the three-slot form is wanted.

> The draft becomes the one the spec describes — composition requirements compile into a round
> schedule that types the team slots, players bid priority cards for turn order over that visible
> schedule, and swaps can only take something the target slot allows.

**Slice delivered by this plan:** the group reaches round 1 of a two-Mega tournament and the pool
holds only Pokémon that can still Mega, with `Round 1 is a Mega round — only Pokémon that can still
Mega are shown.` beside the count. Round 3 opens back up. The finished team exports with a stone on
the Mega slots and no stone anywhere else.

<objective>
Make a Mega round mean something at the table, and make the slot decide the export.

Purpose: this is where "the compiler deliberately removes runtime validation" is cashed in. There
is no post-pick check — the offer is constrained instead, so an illegal pick is unreachable rather
than rejected. The same slot type that constrains the offer is what puts a stone on the export.

Output: the round restriction composed into `matchesFilters`, `selectRoundEligibleIds`, the pool
header's statement and three empty states, and `selectSlotStone` feeding the export path.
</objective>
