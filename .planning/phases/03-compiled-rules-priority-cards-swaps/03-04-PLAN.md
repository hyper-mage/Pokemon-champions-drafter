> The draft becomes the one the spec describes — composition requirements compile into a round
> schedule that types the team slots, players bid priority cards for turn order over that visible
> schedule, and swaps can only take something the target slot allows.

**Slice delivered by this plan:** the host scrolls a grid of all 76 Mega formes, bans
`Charizard-Mega-X` by name or by clicking its cell, sees `1 of 76 Mega formes banned`, and the
tournament records that ban — with Charizard-Mega-Y still legal.

the failure both `search.ts:184-195` and `feasibility.ts:8-9` already have comments preventing.

Output: `src/core/mega.ts`, the render path widened so a Mega forme is a first-class pool cell, and
the `Mega-forme bans` sub-section inside the `Mega rules` group.
</objective>
